🌎 Geopolitics & World Affairs
Bennett and Lapid Formalize Alliance — With Netanyahu Squarely in Their Sights

Former Israeli prime ministers Naftali Bennett and Yair Lapid have agreed to merge their political parties, forming a unified opposition bloc aimed directly at unseating Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu. The move consolidates two of Israel’s most prominent centrist and center-right figures into a single electoral force. Netanyahu’s corruption trial, meanwhile, continues to be disrupted — testimony was again canceled over security concerns. (AP News, Times of Israel)

Why it matters: A credible, unified opposition significantly shifts Israel’s political calculus ahead of any future election, threatening Netanyahu’s grip on power at one of the most turbulent moments in the country’s recent history.

Shooting at the White House Correspondents’ Dinner — New Details Emerge

Video has surfaced showing the suspected gunman physically running through a metal detector and toward the ballroom at Saturday’s White House Correspondents’ Dinner, where President Trump was seated. Security failures are now under intense scrutiny, as the footage raises serious questions about how the suspect breached multiple checkpoints. A Wisconsin brewery also drew public backlash after a Facebook post appeared to celebrate the assassination attempt. (Newsweek, CBS News)

Why it matters: A security breach at one of Washington’s most high-profile annual events — with the sitting president in the room — will trigger a major overhaul of protective protocols and a fierce political and cultural debate about political violence.

EU and US Launch Critical Minerals Partnership to Outflank China

The European Union and the United States formalized a new critical minerals partnership in April 2026, signing a memorandum of understanding and an Action Plan designed to reduce dependency on China-dominated supply chains. The agreement spans the full minerals value chain, from extraction to processing, and is explicitly framed around coordinating defence and EV manufacturing supply lines. (SLDinfo.com)

Why it matters: As China tightens its grip on rare earth exports, a transatlantic minerals bloc could reshape the economics of clean energy and defence manufacturing for the next decade — if it moves from memoranda to mines.

 
🤖 AI & Technology
China Blocks Meta’s Acquisition of AI Startup Manus

Chinese regulators have moved to block Meta’s planned acquisition of Manus, a fast-rising Chinese AI startup that gained global attention earlier this year for its autonomous agent capabilities. Beijing cited national security and technology transfer concerns in reversing the deal. The move signals China’s growing assertiveness in treating homegrown AI as a strategic asset off-limits to US acquirers. (AP News, Washington Post)

Why it matters: The block illustrates how AI acquisitions are now entangled in geopolitical rivalry — and puts US tech firms on notice that Chinese AI talent and IP will increasingly be walled off, even when founders are willing sellers.

Microsoft Trims OpenAI Revenue Share — The Partnership Quietly Shifts

Microsoft has cut the revenue share it pays to OpenAI as part of a deliberate strategy to loosen its dependency on the ChatGPT maker, according to reporting from AP News. The renegotiation reflects Microsoft’s ambition to build more independent AI capabilities through Copilot and its own model investments, rather than remaining a de facto reseller of OpenAI’s technology. The move comes amid broader signs that the two companies are evolving from tight partners toward cautious rivals.

Why it matters: A looser Microsoft-OpenAI alliance reshapes the competitive landscape for enterprise AI — potentially benefiting Anthropic, Google, and others competing for the same corporate contracts.

US State Department Warns World About DeepSeek’s Alleged AI Data Theft

The US State Department has issued a global advisory warning allied governments about alleged intellectual property theft by DeepSeek and other Chinese AI firms, urging partners to restrict or scrutinize deployments. The warning escalates Washington’s campaign to constrain Chinese AI on the international stage, going beyond domestic bans to lobbying allies directly. (iTnews)

Why it matters: If allies follow Washington’s lead, DeepSeek’s international expansion could face a coordinated wall of restrictions — a significant blow to China’s ambition to position its AI models as a global alternative to US platforms.

 
💼 Business & Finance
S&P 500’s Best Month Since 2020 — and the Doubters Are Getting Louder

Global equities enter the week at or near record highs, with the S&P 500 up nearly 10% since the end of March — putting it on pace for its best monthly performance since late 2020. Treasury yields fell Friday after the Justice Department closed its probe into Fed Chair Jerome Powell, clearing a path for Kevin Warsh’s confirmation and stoking bets on earlier rate cuts. But strategists are flagging the rally’s speed as a red flag, with momentum outrunning fundamentals. (Bloomberg)

Why it matters: A 10% monthly surge built partly on rate-cut speculation and political resolution at the Fed is fragile — any earnings disappointment this week could hand the doubters their moment.

China’s Industrial Profits Surge 15.5% — Even as the Oil Shock Bites

China’s industrial profits climbed 15.5% in the first quarter of 2026 — the strongest Q1 pickup in five years — driven by a reflation wave that has offset rising energy costs from the ongoing Hormuz disruption. March alone saw profits jump 15.8% year-on-year, beating Bloomberg Economics expectations. The headline figure, however, masks a widening split between export-oriented manufacturers struggling with tariffs and domestic-facing industries riding the reflation wave. (Bloomberg)

Why it matters: Strong Chinese industrial profits reduce pressure on Beijing to launch massive stimulus — but the internal split suggests the economy’s resilience is unevenly distributed and vulnerable to any further trade escalation.

 
🏀 Sports
Kevin Durant Sits Again — Rockets Face Elimination in Game 4 Against the Lakers

Kevin Durant is sidelined once more as the Houston Rockets head into a must-win Game 4 against the Los Angeles Lakers, facing playoff elimination down 3–0 in the series. Durant’s absence has crippled Houston’s offensive ceiling at the worst possible moment. (AP News)

Why it matters: If the Lakers sweep, it confirms their status as the Western Conference’s most dangerous team — and raises serious questions about Houston’s roster construction around an injury-prone Durant.

Madrid Open Rolls On: Blockx vs. Auger-Aliassime Takes Center Court

The 2026 Mutua Madrid Open continues Monday with Alexandre Blockx facing Felix Auger-Aliassime in a compelling matchup on the clay. The tournament has already delivered upsets after Griekspoor knocked out Musetti earlier in the draw. (Sky Sports)

Why it matters: Madrid is the last major clay tune-up before Rome, making every result a critical data point for who arrives at Roland Garros with momentum — and who doesn’t.
