📈 Markets & Finance
Wall Street Cruises Toward Eight Straight Winning Weeks as Bond Pressure Eases

U.S. stocks rose Friday as Treasury yields pulled back from their recent highs, removing the headwind that had weighed on equities throughout the week. The broad advance brought major indexes within striking distance of record territory, capping a remarkably resilient stretch for American markets. (AP, The Sun Chronicle)

Why it matters: Eight consecutive winning weeks would be the longest such streak in years—a signal that investor confidence remains robust even as inflation worries and geopolitical risks linger on the edges.

Nvidia Gets a "Yawn" — Then the Rally Resumes Anyway

Nvidia’s latest blowout earnings report was met with a muted initial reaction on Wall Street before the AI rally picked back up, with Asian shares following suit and rising for a second consecutive session. Investors rotated into a broader basket of AI-infrastructure names rather than concentrating solely on the chip giant. (AP, Bloomberg, Investopedia)

Why it matters: When even a record-breaking Nvidia report barely moves the needle, it suggests the AI trade has matured—and that the broader ecosystem of data centers, energy, and networking stocks is where the next gains may come from.

Estée Lauder and Puig Perfume Marriage Called Off

Beauty giant Estée Lauder and Spanish fragrance house Puig—owner of Charlotte Tilbury—have ended merger talks, according to a Friday report. The deal would have been one of the largest in the luxury beauty sector, potentially reshaping the competitive landscape against L’Oréal and LVMH. (AP)

Why it matters: Estée Lauder, still recovering from post-pandemic sales slumps, loses a potential lifeline for growth—expect renewed scrutiny of its standalone strategy from investors.

 
🌎 Geopolitics & World Affairs
Iran War Uncertainty Keeps Oil Markets on Edge, Asia Rattled

Oil prices climbed Friday on continued uncertainty surrounding the U.S.-Iran conflict, reversing a brief dip that had briefly lifted markets the day before. Asian shares still managed gains on optimism around Iran diplomatic talks, but analysts warn any breakdown in negotiations could send crude sharply higher. (AP, Bloomberg)

Why it matters: Oil price volatility tied to the Iran conflict is the single biggest macro wildcard right now—a spike toward $100/barrel would rekindle inflation fears and complicate central bank decisions globally.

Ukraine Steps Up Deep-Strike Campaign, Hitting Russian Refinery

Ukraine said its drones struck another oil refinery deep inside Russian territory as its long-range campaign intensifies in 2026. A Washington-based think tank assessed that the sustained strikes have degraded Russia’s ability to sustain offensive operations, supporting recent Ukrainian battlefield advances. Meanwhile, Kyiv protesters gathered to oppose a bill that families fear could prematurely declare missing soldiers legally dead. (AP)

Why it matters: Ukraine’s targeting of Russian energy infrastructure is a deliberate economic pressure strategy—but the domestic political tension over the "missing soldiers" bill shows the war’s human cost is straining social cohesion at home.

Quad Foreign Ministers Set to Convene in New Delhi

Japan confirmed that top diplomats from the United States, Japan, Australia, and India will hold a Quad foreign ministers’ meeting in Delhi, a significant gathering of the Indo-Pacific’s key democratic powers. The meeting comes as China-Russia ties deepen following the Putin-Xi summit in Beijing earlier this week. (Reuters)

Why it matters: The Quad convening now is no coincidence—it’s a direct diplomatic counterweight to the Beijing summit, and the agenda is expected to address regional security, supply chains, and the Iran situation’s ripple effects across Asia.

 
🤖 Science & Technology
Trump Pumps the Brakes on White House AI Executive Order

President Trump has delayed signing a landmark AI executive order, citing concerns that its provisions could amount to overregulation and risk ceding ground to China in the global AI race. Industry and safety experts had hoped the order would address cybersecurity vulnerabilities and novel risks including bioweapons and autonomous AI development. (The Washington Post)

Why it matters: The delay leaves a regulatory vacuum at precisely the moment AI capabilities are advancing fastest—companies get more runway, but so do bad actors exploiting the absence of federal guardrails.

Google’s AI Mode Ads Arrive—and Brands Are Nervous

Google has rolled out new ad formats embedded within its AI Mode search experience, requiring brands to trust the platform’s algorithms to place and contextualise their messages within AI-generated answers. Advertisers are wrestling with reduced control over where and how their brand appears—a tension that could reshape digital marketing budgets. (Ad Age)

Why it matters: Google’s AI Mode is rapidly becoming the default search experience for millions—how brands adapt their trust in automation here will set the template for the next era of digital advertising.
