Greece Unveils Parthenon's Restored West Pediment in Most Complete Form Since 1801

On June 18, 2026, Greece's Culture Ministry revealed the Parthenon's restored west pediment atop the Acropolis in Athens, presenting the ancient temple's western façade in its most complete form in more than 200 years. The restoration centred on the placement of two new stones, which Culture Minister Lina Mendoni described as doing more than filling a structural gap — restoring the building's celebrated geometric proportions. Mendoni called the result "truly stunning." The milestone marks a significant step in the long-running Acropolis restoration programme, which has been ongoing for decades. (AP News)

Why it matters: The restoration returns one of humanity's most iconic ancient monuments closer to its classical appearance for the first time in over two centuries, with direct implications for cultural heritage preservation and Greece's ongoing campaign for the return of the Elgin Marbles.
