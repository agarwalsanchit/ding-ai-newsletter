Putin Signals Escalation as Ukraine Stalemate Deepens; Zelensky Warns of Large-Scale Russian Attack

🌍 Geopolitics & World Affairs
Shangri-La Dialogue Opens in Singapore with China and US Reliability Dominating the Agenda

The Shangri-La Dialogue, the International Institute for Strategic Studies' annual Asia-Pacific defense and security summit, opened in Singapore on May 28, 2026. AP reporting indicates the summit opened with two dominant concerns: China's growing military assertiveness and deepening uncertainty about US priorities under the Trump administration, including questions about Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth's engagement with the region. The forum brings together defense ministers, military chiefs, and senior officials from across the Asia-Pacific and beyond, and serves as a key venue for bilateral meetings and public signaling on security issues. Vietnam was also cited as a point of discussion. (AP News)

Why it matters: The Shangri-La Dialogue sets the tone for Asia-Pacific security diplomacy for the year ahead, and the dual focus on Chinese assertiveness and US reliability gaps signals a region actively hedging its strategic bets.
