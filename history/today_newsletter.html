President Donald Trump signaled on Sunday that the United States and Iran are inching closer to a deal, describing peace talks as proceeding while cautioning that significant differences remain between the two sides. Why it matters: Even tentative US-Iran diplomatic progress is moving oil markets and reducing immediate conflict risk around the Strait of Hormuz, a chokepoint for roughly 20% of global oil supply — meaning a deal or breakdown will have direct consequences for global energy prices and regional stability.
