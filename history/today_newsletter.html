Artemis II Blasts Off — First Humans Bound for the Moon in Over 50 Years

The Artemis II crew launched successfully from Kennedy Space Center in Florida, marking the first crewed mission to the Moon since Apollo 17 in 1972, with NASA teams and onlookers watching in awe, according to ABC World News Tonight via Modern Ghana. The Orion capsule is now en route, carrying four astronauts on what is expected to be a lunar flyby mission before a future landing attempt. The launch comes amid the backdrop of war and economic strain, offering a rare moment of shared national spectacle.

Why it matters: Beyond the symbolism, Artemis II is the critical crewed dress rehearsal that validates every system needed for an actual lunar landing — its success or failure will define the pace of the entire US return-to-Moon timeline.
