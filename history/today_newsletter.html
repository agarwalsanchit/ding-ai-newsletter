Michael Dell Overtakes Larry Ellison as World's Fifth-Richest Person After Dell Stock Surge

Dell Technologies shares rose nearly 4% on Wednesday, June 18, 2026, closing at one of their highest levels of the year. The move underscores how closely tied individual billionaire fortunes are to daily stock market fluctuations, particularly among major technology company founders whose wealth is heavily concentrated in their own firms' equity. (forbes.com)
