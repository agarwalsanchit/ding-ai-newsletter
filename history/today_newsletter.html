UN Reports Record 118 Million People Displaced by Conflict and Persecution in 2025

The United Nations reported on June 11, 2026 that nearly 118 million people were displaced by conflict and persecution in the previous year, marking a record high. The AP report by Renata Brito coincides with images of Lebanese families fleeing southern villages, underscoring the ongoing human toll of regional conflicts. The figure reflects a continued upward trend in global displacement, driven by a combination of armed conflicts, political persecution, and instability across multiple continents. The UN data puts renewed pressure on host countries and international bodies to expand humanitarian response capacity. (AP News)

Why it matters: A record 118 million displaced persons strains international humanitarian systems and signals that existing global conflict-resolution and refugee-support frameworks are failing to keep pace with the scale of human suffering. The figure will likely intensify political debates in host nations over migration policy and burden-sharing.
