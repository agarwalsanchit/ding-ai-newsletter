Russia Pounds Kyiv with Missiles and Drones, Injuring 10

Russia struck Kyiv with a combined missile and drone barrage in the early hours of Sunday, May 24, injuring at least 10 people and causing damage in the city center. A simultaneous attack on Balakliia in the Kharkiv region ignited a prayer house belonging to a local Evangelical Christian Baptist community. Ukrainian emergency services responded to both scenes. The strikes come amid continued Russian military pressure across Ukraine, with recent reporting indicating Moscow is seeking a war victory by year-end. (AP News)

Why it matters: Continued large-scale strikes on Kyiv and civilian infrastructure signal Russia is intensifying pressure on Ukraine's population and morale ahead of any potential ceasefire negotiations, with direct humanitarian and diplomatic consequences.

On May 20, 2026, a cluster of AI-industry milestones prompted observers to label the moment a potential inflection point. Why it matters: Nvidia's $81.6 billion quarter and Anthropic's $10.9 billion revenue projection illustrate that AI infrastructure spending has moved beyond hype into sustained, historic capital flows. Vietnam's Dien May Xanh Cleared for $545 Million IPO

Dien May Xanh, the electronics and home appliance retail arm of Vietnam's Mobile World Group (MWG), has won regulatory approval for an initial public offering valued at approximately $545 million. The IPO comes as Vietnam's expanding middle class drives rising consumer demand and interest in wealth accumulation. The listing is expected to be one of the larger offerings in Vietnam's capital markets in recent years, reflecting broader momentum in the country's retail and financial sectors. (The Investor)

Why it matters: A $545 million IPO for one of Vietnam's best-known retail chains would be a landmark transaction for Southeast Asian capital markets and signals growing investor confidence in Vietnam's consumer economy. It also highlights the country's emergence as a destination for both retail investment and foreign manufacturing capital.
